GUIDE — HOW IT WORKS

  • Chat: open the bot and send any message. It routes through n8n to the Dolphin model on the CR8MOR Ollama and replies in the same chat.
  • Inline: in any Telegram chat, type @hollemu_bot followed by your question to pull the bot in without leaving the conversation.
  • Brain: dolphin-llama3:8b (Dolphin-class, uncensored) on the Zeabur Ollama, wired through the n8n workflow “Telegram Bot to Ollama (hollemu)”.
  • Privacy: the model is local-stack. Your messages go to the bot and the model — not to a public AI service.
